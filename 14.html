Vegetarian Recipes

Healthy and delicious vegetarian meals.

Vegetable Salad

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot, lettuce and lemon.

Preparation: Chop vegetables and mix with lemon juice.

Calories: Approximately 150 kcal

Healthy Green Bowl

Ingredients: Broccoli, spinach, beans and olive oil.

Preparation: Steam vegetables and serve in a bowl.

Calories: Approximately 220 kcal

Mixed Vegetable Soup

Ingredients: Carrot, beans, tomato and pepper.

Preparation: Boil vegetables and add mild spices.

Calories: Approximately 120 kcal

Non-Vegetarian Recipes

Protein-rich meals for a balanced diet.

Grilled Chicken

Ingredients: Chicken breast, pepper, lemon and garlic.

Preparation: Marinate chicken and grill until fully cooked.

Calories: Approximately 280 kcal

Grilled Fish

Ingredients: Fish, lemon, garlic, pepper and herbs.

Preparation: Season fish and grill until fully cooked.

Calories: Approximately 240 kcal

Boiled Eggs

Ingredients: Eggs, water and a little salt.

Preparation: Boil eggs until fully cooked.

Calories: Approximately 155 kcal (2 eggs)

Chicken Curry

Ingredients: Chicken, onion, tomato, ginger and spices.

Preparation: Cook chicken thoroughly with the curry ingredients.

Calories: Approximately 300 kcal

Healthy Eating Tips

Follow these simple habits for a healthier lifestyle.

Drink Enough Water

Drink water regularly throughout the day.

Eat Fruits

Include fresh fruits in your daily diet.

Eat Protein

Include eggs, fish, chicken, pulses and dairy products.

Avoid Junk Food

Limit highly processed foods and sugary drinks.

Balanced Diet

Eat vegetables, fruits, protein and whole grains.

Exercise Regularly

Stay physically active and get enough sleep.
